## 🙋 Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient with other people, especially newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Own your mistakes, and learn from them.

## 🚫 Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in any form.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 🎯 Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and review comments. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## 🆘 Reporting

Send reports privately to the maintainer through [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/vaibhavmahna). Include what happened, where, and who was involved. Links or screenshots help.

Reports are handled privately. The maintainers will not share the reporter's identity beyond what is needed to act on the report.

## 🔨 Enforcement

Maintainers review each report and decide on a response. Depending on severity, that can be a private note, a public warning, a temporary block, or a permanent ban from project spaces. The Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) describe each level.
